# Store Listing Copy

Paste directly into the store consoles. Every field is within its character limit.
Keywords target real search intent for what the app does (barcode scanner, barcode country /
product origin, brand owner, boycott / ethical shopping). Don't add QR-code keywords: the app
reads retail barcodes (EAN/UPC), not QR codes, and irrelevant keywords break both stores'
metadata rules and hurt ranking through uninstalls.

# Google Play

Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

```
Solidarity: Product Origin
```

## Short description (max 80 characters)

```
Barcode scanner: see a product's country & brand owner. Boycott or support it.
```

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Solidarity is the barcode scanner for conscious shoppers. Scan any product and see the country behind its barcode, who owns the brand, and what the community says: boycott it or support it.

Wondering where a product comes from? Every retail barcode starts with a GS1 prefix that shows the country where the barcode was registered. Solidarity reads it in a second, a quick clue to a product's origin. Registration country isn't always where a product was made, and the app always tells you so.

WHY PEOPLE USE SOLIDARITY

• Boycott or support with confidence. Set your own Avoid / Support rules for countries, brands and parent companies. Your rules lead every scan result.
• Find out who owns a brand. See the parent company behind the label, so you know who you're really paying.
• Swap it. When a product is one to avoid, Solidarity suggests similar products you've already found fine.
• A community verdict, not a black box. Every country's status comes from open, transparent votes by real users. No hidden lists.

FEATURES

📷 Barcode scanner
Scan EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and UPC-E barcodes with your camera, type the number, or check a barcode from a photo or screenshot. Check digits catch misreads and typos. Torch for dark aisles.

🛒 Basket mode
Scan your whole shopping cart and get one sorted list: avoid, fine and neutral.

🏷️ Product names and brand owners
Optional product lookup from Open Food Facts shows the product name, the brand and the company that owns it.

✅ Avoid / Support rules
Personal rules for countries, brands and companies. They stay on your device.

🗳️ Community votes
Upvote or downvote any country, change your vote any time, and see live tallies across 115 countries.

🕘 Scan history
Search and filter everything you've scanned by country, product, barcode or date.

🏆 Streaks, XP and badges
Level up as you scan and vote, keep your streak alive and unlock badges. Optional global leaderboard.

📤 Share your stats
Turn your level, streak and badges into a shareable image.

PRIVACY FIRST

Barcode scanning happens on your device and your scan history stays on your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. An account is optional, and you can delete it and its data from inside the app at any time.

WORKS ON EVERY SCREEN

Phones, tablets and foldables, in portrait and landscape.

Solidarity is the barcode scanner for ethical shopping: check a product's country of origin, see the brand owner, and boycott or support with the community. Download it and know what you buy.
```

## Data safety form (matches the privacy policy)

- Data collected (only with an optional account): Email address, Name (display name), User IDs,
  App activity (in-app progress and votes). Purpose: App functionality, Account management.
- Encrypted in transit: Yes. Users can request deletion: Yes (in app and via the URL above).
- Not collected: location, contacts, photos/videos (write-only save), device or advertising IDs.
- Data shared with third parties: No (Firebase is a service provider). No ads.
- Product names (from 1.2, off by default): with the user's opt-in, the app sends the scanned
  barcode number directly to Open Food Facts. A barcode identifies a product, not the user, and
  the transfer is user-initiated after an in-app explanation. That fits Google's exemption for
  sharing the user expects, but review this answer before each release. If in doubt, declare
  "App activity → Other actions" as shared, for App functionality, optional.
