## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Solidarity helps you know exactly what you're buying — scan any product's barcode and instantly see which country it's registered to.

WHAT MAKES SOLIDARITY DIFFERENT

Instead of a developer deciding which countries are "good" or "bad," Solidarity puts that decision in the hands of the community. Every country's status is determined by open, transparent voting from real users — visible to everyone, and updatable by any verified member at any time. No hidden agendas, no arbitrary lists — just a live, crowd-sourced verdict you can trust.

KEY FEATURES

📷 Instant Barcode Scanning
Point your camera at any product barcode (or type it in manually) to identify its GS1 country of registration in seconds.

🗳️ Community Voting
See real-time upvote/downvote counts for every country. Cast your own vote, change it whenever you want, and help keep the community verdict honest and current.

📊 Detailed Stats
A GitHub-style contribution graph tracks your daily scanning activity, alongside your full scan history and positive/negative breakdown.

🏆 Gamification That Actually Rewards You
Earn XP for scanning and voting, level up, build daily streaks, and unlock badges for milestones like your first scan, a 30-day streak, or being an active community voter.

🌍 Community Tab
Browse every country's current vote tally, see what's trending, and manage all of your own votes in one place — even without scanning a single product.

🥇 Leaderboard
Opt in to compete on a global leaderboard ranked by XP. Entirely optional, and only visible to those who choose to join.

📤 Share Your Stats
Generate a beautiful, shareable image of your level, streak, and badges to post on social media.

PRIVACY FIRST

Scanning always happens on-device — your barcode history and scan data never leave your phone. If you optionally create an account, only a small aggregated summary (your level, streak, badges, and vote choices) is backed up to the cloud, purely so you don't lose your progress. Accounts are completely optional; the app works fully offline without one.

Download Solidarity today and start making informed choices, backed by a transparent, community-driven verdict — not a black box.
```
