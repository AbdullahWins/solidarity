Delete your Solidarity account

App: Solidarity (com.abdullahwins.solidarity) · Developer: NADESOFT

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time. You don't need an account to use Solidarity, so if you never created one, there's nothing stored with us.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Solidarity and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete.

Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

Email contact@nadesoft.bd from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Solidarity account”. We'll confirm the request and delete your account and data within 14 days. If you can no longer use that email address, tell us and we'll help verify that the account is yours.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account (email, password, display name)Deleted
Progress backup (XP, level, streaks, badges, daily counts)Deleted
Leaderboard entryDeleted
Your votesDeleted, and subtracted from each country's public total
Anonymous vote totalsKept. They don't identify you and no longer include your votes
Google Firebase encrypted backupsExpire automatically, normally within 30 days
Scan history on your phoneNot stored by us. Remove it with Settings → Clear scan history or by uninstalling the app

Delete only some data

  • Leave the leaderboard: Profile → turn off Show me on the leaderboard.
  • Remove a vote: tap your current vote on that country again.
  • Clear scans on this device: Settings → Clear scan history.
