Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Solidarity app for Android and iOS, and this website.

The short version
  • No account? Nothing is collected. Scanning works fully offline, and your scan history never leaves your device.
  • Product names are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open product database, to look the product up.
  • With an optional account we store your email, display name, a summary of your progress and your votes, using Google Firebase.
  • We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your account and all its cloud data in the app at any time (Profile → Delete account).

1. Who we are

Solidarity is made by NADESOFT ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at contact@nadesoft.bd.

4. What we don't collect

No advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no advertising ID, no location, no contacts, no microphone, and no reading of your photo library. Notifications are scheduled on your device; we have no push server and don't collect a push token.

5. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • Providing your account, backup, votes and leaderboard: needed to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • Looking up product names: your consent, given by turning on Product names. Turn it off at any time.
  • Showing you on the public leaderboard: your consent, given by turning on the leaderboard switch. Turn it off at any time.
  • Keeping the service secure and preventing abuse: our legitimate interest in running a reliable, fair service.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions that affect you.

6. Where it's stored

Account and cloud data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes data for us under the Firebase data processing terms. Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers are covered by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

7. Who can see it

  • Everyone can see each country's total up and down vote counts. These counts are anonymous and don't show who voted.
  • Everyone can see your leaderboard entry (display name, XP, level and streaks), but only if you opted in.
  • Only you can read your progress backup and your individual votes. Our database security rules enforce this.
  • Older app versions: until everyone has updated past version 1.0.2, other signed-in users of those old versions can also read the progress backup (activity counts and vote choices) of users who have opted in to the leaderboard. Turning the leaderboard off removes this.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase, as described above.
  • Open Food Facts, only if you turn on Product names: the app sends the barcode number straight from your device to Open Food Facts, an independent non-profit open database, which also sees your IP address as with any web request. We don't send it your account, location or scan history. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy. Turn it off in Settings → Product names.
  • Legal requests: we may disclose data if the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.

8. How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. Daily scan counts in your backup cover only the last 120 days. When you delete your account, the data is deleted immediately from our live database. Google may keep it in encrypted backups for a limited time, normally up to 30 days. Anonymous vote totals don't identify you; your own votes are subtracted from them when you delete your account.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email contact@nadesoft.bd and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU, EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you live in California, you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We don't sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

10. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account, then confirm with your password. This deletes your account, your progress backup, your leaderboard entry and all your votes, which are also removed from the public counts. Scans on your device stay until you clear them (Settings → Clear scan history) or uninstall the app. You can also request deletion without the app.

11. Permissions

  • Camera: to read barcodes. Images are processed on your device and never saved or uploaded. You can type barcodes in instead.
  • Add to photos: only when you tap Save on your stats image. The app can't read your photo library.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on streak reminders.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never seen by us. Database rules limit each person to their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

13. Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account, or older if your country requires it (up to 16 in parts of the EU). Scanning without an account collects nothing and is fine at any age. If you think a child under the minimum age has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

14. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or external fonts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
