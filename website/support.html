Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? We read every message.

Email support
Reporting a bug? Please include your device model, Android or iOS version, the app version (bottom of Settings), what you were doing, and the barcode number if it's about a scan.

Scanning

Why does it say “registered in” and not “made in”?

The first digits of a barcode (the GS1 prefix) show the country of the organisation that registered the barcode. Companies often register barcodes where their head office is, so a product can be made somewhere else.

The camera won't read a barcode.

Hold the phone 10–20 cm away so the whole barcode fits inside the frame, keep it steady, and turn on the torch (top right of the camera) in low light. Very curved, damaged or tiny barcodes can be hard to read. You can always type the number instead: tap Type a code instead under the camera.

Which barcodes work?

Retail barcodes: EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and UPC-E. QR codes and shipping or internal codes aren't supported.

It says “check digit doesn't match”.

The last digit of a barcode is a checksum. This message means a digit was mistyped. Compare the number with the one printed under the bars.

Some results say “Reserved” or “Coupons”, and I can't vote.

Some number ranges aren't countries. They're used for in-store codes, coupons, books (ISBN) or magazines (ISSN), so there's nothing to vote on.

I denied camera access by mistake.

Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Solidarity → Permissions and allow Camera, or tap Open Settings on the Scan screen.

How do product names work?

Turn on Settings → Product names (or answer the prompt on your first scan). The app then sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, open product database, and shows the name, brand and photo it finds. Results are saved on your phone, so repeat scans work offline. If the product is wrong or missing, use Not the right product? or Add it to fix it on Open Food Facts for everyone.

What are “My country rules”?

Open a country (or Details & vote on a scan result) and choose Avoid or Support. Your rule then leads every scan from that country, with the community verdict shown underneath. Rules stay on your phone only. Manage them in Settings → My country rules.

Voting and the community

How are country colours decided?

By open voting. A country is shown as positive when it has at least as many up votes as down votes, and negative otherwise. Anyone can see the counts on the Community tab.

Why can't I vote?

Voting needs an account with a verified email. Sign in from Profile, then open the link we emailed you and tap I've verified.

How do I change or remove my vote?

Tap the other button to change your vote, or tap your current vote again to remove it. You can manage all your votes under Vote → My votes.

Account and sync

Do I need an account?

No. Scanning, stats, streaks and badges all work without one. An account adds a cloud backup, voting and the optional leaderboard.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a reset link.

I didn't get the verification email.

Check your spam folder, then tap Resend email in Profile. The link expires after a while, so use the newest one.

I changed phones. Where's my progress?

Sign in on the new phone. Your level, XP, streaks, badges and votes come back from your backup. Your individual scan history is only stored on the old phone, so it doesn't transfer.

How do I appear on (or leave) the leaderboard?

Verify your email, then switch Show me on the leaderboard on or off in Profile. Turning it off removes your entry straight away.

Notifications

How do streak reminders work?

Turn on Settings → Streak reminders. You'll get one reminder at 7 pm, only on days you haven't scanned yet. If you don't see them, check that notifications are allowed for Solidarity in your phone's settings.

Privacy and your data

What data do you keep?

Without an account, nothing. With one, we keep your email, display name, a progress summary and your votes. See the privacy policy for the full list.

How do I delete my scan history?

Settings → Clear scan history. This deletes scans on this device and the XP and badges earned from them.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account, then confirm with your password. Everything is deleted, including your votes. No app? See delete your account.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us from your account's address and we'll send you everything we hold within 30 days.
