Everything you need, nothing you don't

No ads, no tracking, and no account needed to start scanning.

📷

Instant scanning

Point your camera at an EAN or UPC barcode, or type the number in. Check digits are verified, so typos and misreads are caught.

🌍

Registration country

See which country's GS1 prefix the barcode belongs to. That's where the barcode was registered, which isn't always where the product was made.

🗳️

Community verdict

Each country's colour comes from open up and down votes that anyone can see. Verified members can vote, change or remove their vote at any time.

📊

Your stats

A year-long activity graph, streaks, scan history and a shareable stats card. Your scan history stays on your phone.

🏆

Levels and badges

Earn XP for scanning and voting, level up, keep streaks going and unlock badges. The public leaderboard is opt-in.

🔐

Private by design

The app works fully without an account. If you create one, only a progress summary and your votes are backed up.
